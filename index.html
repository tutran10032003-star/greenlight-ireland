A week in the life

Scroll, then tap the button to see how a chore turns into progress.

  1. A parent sets a chore, such as “Tidy your room”.
  2. The child marks it done, the parent approves, and the allowance lands.
  3. The child puts some towards a goal, and the jar fills.
  4. A relative adds birthday money toward the goal.
  5. The child taps the card in a shop, and the parent gets an alert within the limits they set.
